    How the score works

    Every password starts at 0. Mixing character types and adding length raise the score; patterns that attackers try first lower it. Repeated characters and repeated pieces only count once, so aaaa or Ab1!Ab1! add little.

    • Digits, lowercase, uppercase and symbols
    • Runs and keyboard patterns like abcd, 4321, 1357, qwer or 1qaz
    • Several different characters of each type
    • Dates and years like 19881010 or 2024
    • Length, with more points at 8, 12, 16, 20 and 24
    • Common words, even written as p@ssw0rd
    • Three or more different symbols
    • Five characters or fewer, or only a few different ones
    0-3Easy
    4-6Medium
    7-9Strong
    10-12Very strong
    13-14Extremely strong
